Your complete guide to restaurants, bars, and dining in Cave Creek and Carefree, Arizona

Dining in Cave Creek & Carefree, Arizona

Cave Creek and neighboring Carefree offer one of the most distinctive dining experiences in the Phoenix metro area. This isn't your typical suburban restaurant scene — it's a collection of western saloons, ranch-style steakhouses, and locally-owned gems that reflect the area's cowboy heritage and desert character.

With 40 restaurants, bars, and cafes spread across these two communities, you'll find everything from award-winning BBQ and authentic Mexican food to upscale Mediterranean cuisine and cozy Italian trattorias. The vibe here is relaxed, the portions are generous, and the sunsets from patio seating are unbeatable.

What Kind of Food Can You Find in Cave Creek?

Cave Creek's restaurant scene is anchored by American comfort food and western-style dining, but the variety might surprise you. Here's what the local dining landscape looks like:

American & Western Cuisine

With 13 American restaurants, this is the heart of Cave Creek dining. Expect steakhouses with desert views, burger joints with character, and saloons serving ranch-style comfort food. This is the place for a proper ribeye, cold beer, and live country music.

Mexican Restaurants

Six Mexican restaurants serve everything from street-style tacos to full Sonoran platters. The proximity to the border means you're getting authentic flavors — green chile, handmade tortillas, and margaritas made with fresh lime.

Coffee & Cafes

Cave Creek has six coffee spots perfect for a morning caffeine fix before hitting the trails or browsing the local shops. Many offer outdoor seating with mountain views.

Italian & Mediterranean

Five Italian restaurants and two Mediterranean spots bring European flavors to the desert. From wood-fired pizzas to fresh pasta and wine bars with impressive selections.

BBQ & Southwestern

Three BBQ joints and three Southwestern restaurants round out the scene. Slow-smoked brisket, pulled pork, and green chile everything — this is Arizona cooking at its finest.

Best Areas for Dining in Cave Creek

Cave Creek is small enough that you can hit multiple spots in one evening, but knowing the layout helps:

Downtown Cave Creek

The heart of the action. Cave Creek Road is lined with western-themed restaurants, saloons, and shops. This is where you'll find the iconic spots with live music, outdoor patios, and that authentic Arizona cowboy atmosphere.

Carefree

Just east of Cave Creek, Carefree has a slightly more upscale vibe with wine bars, Mediterranean restaurants, and cafes surrounding the famous Carefree Sundial. Great for a quieter dinner or Sunday brunch.

North Scottsdale Border

The southern edge of Cave Creek blends into North Scottsdale, where you'll find some crossover spots that serve both communities.

Tips for Dining in Cave Creek

  • Make reservations on weekends — Popular spots fill up, especially for patio seating during perfect weather months (October through April).
  • Dress casual — This is cowboy country. Jeans and boots fit right in almost everywhere.
  • Plan for sunset — Many restaurants have west-facing patios. Time your dinner to catch the desert sunset.
  • Check for live music — Several saloons and restaurants have live country and western music, especially Thursday through Sunday.
  • Summer hours vary — Some spots reduce hours or close certain days during the hot summer months. Call ahead June through August.
