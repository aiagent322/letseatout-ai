Dining in Scottsdale, Arizona

Scottsdale is Arizona's culinary capital. From celebrity chef restaurants and rooftop lounges to hidden wine bars and legendary steakhouses, this desert city punches well above its weight in the dining department. Whether you're looking for a power lunch, romantic dinner, or late-night bites after hitting Old Town, Scottsdale delivers.

With 142 restaurants, bars, and lounges in our directory, you'll find world-class fine dining alongside neighborhood favorites. The scene here ranges from upscale Mediterranean and French cuisine to craft cocktail bars and innovative breakfast spots that draw weekend crowds.

What Kind of Food Can You Find in Scottsdale?

Scottsdale's restaurant scene is remarkably diverse, reflecting the city's status as a resort destination and affluent community. Here's the breakdown:

Bars & Lounges

With 25 bars and lounges, Scottsdale's nightlife is serious business. From rooftop pools with bottle service to speakeasy-style cocktail dens, the bar scene here rivals much larger cities. Old Town is the epicenter, but you'll find sophisticated spots throughout.

American & New American

24 American restaurants run the gamut from upscale farm-to-table concepts to classic steakhouses. This is where you'll find Scottsdale's most acclaimed chefs pushing boundaries with seasonal menus and local ingredients.

Fine Dining

Scottsdale boasts 12 fine dining establishments — an impressive number for a city this size. Expect tasting menus, extensive wine programs, and the kind of service that justifies a special occasion splurge.

Wine Bars

Eleven wine bars cater to Scottsdale's sophisticated palate. Many feature Arizona wines alongside international selections, with knowledgeable staff and small plates designed for pairing.

Mediterranean & French

Ten Mediterranean restaurants and six French spots bring European elegance to the desert. Fresh seafood, house-made pastas, and classic French techniques are well-represented.

International Flavors

Six Indian restaurants, plus Mexican, Japanese, and Middle Eastern options ensure you're never far from authentic global cuisine. The Indian food scene in particular has developed a strong following.

Best Neighborhoods for Dining in Scottsdale

Old Town Scottsdale

The beating heart of Scottsdale dining and nightlife. This walkable district packs in dozens of restaurants, from casual patios to white-tablecloth destinations. It's where locals and visitors collide, especially on weekend nights.

Scottsdale Waterfront

The area around the Arizona Canal features some of the city's most scenic dining. Upscale restaurants with patio seating overlook the water, making it ideal for date nights and special occasions.

Kierland & North Scottsdale

The northern reaches offer a more suburban feel with excellent dining options. Kierland Commons combines shopping with restaurants, while the surrounding area has neighborhood gems worth seeking out.

South Scottsdale

Closer to Tempe, South Scottsdale tends toward more casual, locally-owned spots. Great for authentic ethnic food and unpretentious neighborhood restaurants.

Tips for Dining in Scottsdale

  • Reserve ahead for fine dining — Top restaurants book up, especially during peak season (January through April) and major events like the Phoenix Open.
  • Happy hour is huge — Many upscale restaurants offer significant discounts during happy hour. It's the local secret for dining well on a budget.
  • Patio season matters — October through May is prime patio weather. Restaurants with good outdoor seating are especially packed during these months.
  • Valet is common — In Old Town especially, valet parking is the norm at nicer restaurants. Factor it into your budget.
  • Dress code varies widely — Old Town nightlife skews dressy, while North Scottsdale is more casual. Check the vibe before showing up in flip-flops.
